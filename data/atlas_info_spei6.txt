Standardised Precipitation-Evapotranspiration Index (SPEI-6)
Monthly drought index that compares the accumulated difference between
precipitation and potential evapotranspiration over a six-month period with the
long-term distribution for the same location and accumulation period, expressed
in units of standard deviations from the mean. Potential evapotranspiration is
estimated using the Hargreaves method (Hargreaves, 1985). The reference period
is 1971-2005 for observations, reanalysis, and projections. Annual values are
calculated as the mean of the monthly values provided by the C3S Atlas Dataset
(https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.h35hb680). Climatologies are calculated as the
mean of the annual time series.
