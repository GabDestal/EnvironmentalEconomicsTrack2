# Abstract

We compare heat and drought distributions in Spain, France, Germany and Sweden
using country-level ERA5-Land indices. Fixed historical thresholds, mean-shift
benchmarks and quantile regressions distinguish changes in overall levels from
additional tail movement. Heat-threshold crossings increase across countries;
a shift in the mean accounts for much of the rise. France and Germany also
show wider pooled temperature distributions, while evidence of extra upper-tail
movement remains imprecise. Drought results vary more across countries and
thresholds. The estimates describe historical changes; small tail samples and
limitations of the early reanalysis record constrain inference.
